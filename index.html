TS 82 CAD Help

It works like AutoCAD: type a command, press Enter or Space, answer the prompt at the bottom. Enter on its own finishes a command, or repeats the last one. Esc cancels. U undoes.

Tips: while a command runs, the bar above the command line says which step it wants and how. Hover over any tool to see what it does. TIPS in the status bar turns the bar off and on.

The bunny: pops up in the bottom corner when something goes wrong (with what to do about it), after a minute with nothing happening (with the next step), and when your plot is clean. Click the bunny to send it away. BUNNY in the status bar turns it off and on.

Week 5: the permeable paver (paver sheet, side 2)

  1. NEW, then Permeable paver. SAVEAS Lastname-paver, in the shared folder.
  2. Layer CUT (red). Your drain hole: CIRCLE Enter, its center like 15,15 Enter, its radius like 6 Enter. Keep it inside the gray square.
  3. Turn 1: ROTATE Enter, W Enter, 0,0 Enter, 32,32 Enter, Enter, 0,0 Enter, C Enter, 90 Enter.
  4. Turn 2: ROTATE Enter, W Enter, -32,0 Enter, 32,32 Enter, Enter, 0,0 Enter, C Enter, 180 Enter.
  5. Count your holes: four of each. Ctrl+S to save. At the laser, follow the laser card with Lastname-paver.dxf.

Week 4 build: the star compass lantern

  1. NEW, then Star compass lantern. Type your W and H, OK. SAVEAS Lastname-lantern, in the shared folder.
  2. MIRROR: click the three slits right of the dashed line, Enter. Click the top end of the dashed line (wait for the green square), then its bottom end. Enter (keep them).
  3. COPY: click just inside the top-left corner of the Hikina panel, then just inside its bottom-right corner (a blue window: 7 found). Enter. Click the strip's bottom-left corner, then @40,0 Enter three times (your W, not 40), Enter.
  4. DIST: click a corner of one slit, then the same corner on the next wall: W. Ctrl+S to save.

Week 4: slide, flip, turn (Week 4 packet page 4)

  1. NEW, then Week 4 triangle. SAVEAS Lastname-week4, in the shared folder.
  2. Slide: click the triangle, then the letters A, B and C. COPY Enter, click corner C, @70,20 Enter, Enter.
  3. Flip: click the triangle, A, B and C. MIRROR Enter, 0,0 Enter, 50,0 Enter, N Enter.
  4. Turn: click the triangle, A, B and C. ROTATE Enter, 0,0 Enter, C Enter, 90 Enter.
  5. A side: DIST Enter, click one corner, then the next. An angle: MEA Enter, A Enter, click one side of the angle, then the other side; Esc when done. A corner's x and y: ID Enter, click it.
  6. Ctrl+S to save.

Draw your plate (Week 3 packet page 3)

  1. Type SAVEAS and name it Lastname-plate, in the shared folder.
  2. Layer SCORE (blue). LINE 20,45 Enter @40,0 Enter, Enter. That is AB.
  3. CIRCLE 20,45 Enter 40 Enter: the circle at A.
  4. CIRCLE 60,45 Enter 40 Enter: the circle at B.
  5. LINE: click the top crossing, then the bottom one (wait for the green X). Enter.
  6. TRIM: click the 4 outside pieces of the circles, 3 on the left circle and 1 on the right. Enter.
  7. Layer DRAWN (gray). CIRCLE 92,65 Enter 10 Enter: the HOLE.
  8. MIRROR: click the HOLE, Enter, click A, click B, N Enter: the PEG.
  9. Ctrl+S to save.

Offset it with your kerf (Week 3 packet page 4)

  1. Layer CUT (red). OFFSET Enter, L Enter, C Enter, then your offset (like 0.1) Enter.
  2. Click the gray outline, then click outside it. Click the HOLE, then inside it. Click the PEG, then outside it. Enter.
  3. Zoom in: red outside the gray, except inside on the HOLE. Ctrl+S.

Typing points, as in AutoCAD with dynamic input on

20,45a first point: 20 across, 45 up from 0,0
@40,040 across from the last point (any point after the first works this way even without the @)
#60,45the absolute point 60,45, even after the first point
4040 long in the direction of the cursor (direct distance; ORTHO keeps it square)

Mouse

Wheel zooms. Middle button drags to pan; double-click it to zoom extents. Shift-drag also pans. Click objects to select them before a command, or click empty space twice for a window. Right-click is Enter.

Saving

In Chrome and Edge, Save As asks where to put the file, and Save (Ctrl+S) writes over it, as in AutoCAD. Firefox and Safari put every save in Downloads; hand in the newest one. Plot makes the laser file the same way.

Your work also keeps itself in this browser between visits. A saved file is what opens on another computer and in AutoCAD.
